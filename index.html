Frequently asked

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The Library, Safety, Tools, and Breeds sections work fully offline. Only the Cat Photo Reader needs an internet connection.

My purchase was interrupted — how do I get my reading?

Reopen the Photo tab. If a purchase completed but the message didn't unlock, the app automatically shows a "Retry unlock (already paid)" button — one tap retrieves it at no extra charge. Each Full Cat Reading is a one-time unlock for that photo. If anything looks wrong, email us and we'll make it right.

Is the Cat Photo Reader real advice?

No — it's an entertainment feature that writes a playful, imaginative message from your cat's point of view. It isn't behavioral, medical, or veterinary advice. For any health concern, please consult a licensed veterinarian.

Is the care information reliable?

The Library and Safety sections cite trusted sources such as the Cornell Feline Health Center, ASPCA, Pet Poison Helpline, International Cat Care, and WSAVA. They are for education and reference, not a substitute for veterinary care.
